# Estate UAT Gap Analysis — M07 / M08 / M09 (money verticals)

Source spec: `Estate_Visitor_Access_UAT_Workplan_Test_Cases.md` (20 Oct 2026 draft, lines 1346–1847).
Repo inspected at working tree of `/Users/paymax/Desktop/wordpress/spotlight/new`. All paths relative to repo root.

Verdict rubric (per task): a case counts **Implemented** only if the ledger posting AND the guard both exist. Money invariants checked: idempotent payment (no double-charge on replay), correct liability/settlement account, partial allocation, late fees, refund dual control, settlement to right beneficiary, reconciliation, server-side authorization.

## What actually exists (baseline)

- **Dues money path (Block 29)**: manual per-resident invoice + full-amount settlement from wallet or Paystack-funded rail, balanced double-entry into the shared finance ledger (`estate_dues_invoices`, `estate_payments`, `estate_dues_restrictions`, `estate_audit_log` — `supabase/migrations/20260623120000_estate_dues_money_path.sql`).
- **Paystack dues checkout**: initiate + status-poll + shared webhook confirmation (`backend/internal/estate/paystackcheckout/`, wired at `backend/internal/app/finance_routes.go:1404–1415`, `backend/internal/webhooks/webhooks.go:352–358`, adapter `finance_routes.go:3627–3640`).
- **Ledger primitives**: TOCTOU-safe debit under advisory lock with identity-verified replay, guarded journals, reversal pair, standing accounts (`backend/internal/finance/ledger/repository.go:240–352, 404–566`, `service.go:108–303`).
- **Property/tenancy**: landlord/tenant assignment, occupancy, transfer workflow, tenancy requests with lease dates (`backend/internal/estate/property_mgmt.go`, `service.go:1448–1546`, `supabase/migrations/20260623150000_estate_property_management.sql`). **There is NO rent billing engine, no deposit model, no lease entity with rent terms.**
- **Vendor payout**: idempotent wallet credit DR settlement / CR vendor wallet (`backend/internal/estate/vendor.go:283–339`).
- **Dues admin reads**: reconciliation projection + payments/invoices/restrictions lists (`backend/internal/app/estate_admin_routes.go:284–379`).

## Highest-severity money gaps (P0 blockers)

1. **No partial/over allocation anywhere** (0129/0147/0171/0172) — `service_dues.go:239–242` forces exact-amount settlement; there is no outstanding-balance model, so every multi-tender/partial scenario fails by construction.
2. **No refund surface** (0175/0176/0177) — gateway refund only fires inside failed-checkout auto-reversal; no request/approval/dual control, no per-payment refund cap; ledger `PostReversal` never wired to estate.
3. **No settlement-to-beneficiary rails** (0178/0180) — estate revenue accumulates in one platform-level `settlement` standing account, settled "out of band"; vendor bank accounts are stored but never verified or used.
4. **No lease/rent ledger** (M08 overall) — tenancy rows carry dates only; no rent invoices, deposits, late fees, renewals or terminations; the entire rent liability account is unbuilt.
5. **No assessment engine, penalties, periods or waivers** (M07 core: 0116–0124, 0136–0137) — billing is manual single-invoice inserts with no idempotency (0123 duplicate-run risk is live), no late-fee postings, no locked periods, no write-off journal.

Mitigating strength: the paths that DO exist (wallet/Paystack dues settlement, webhook confirmation, vendor payout) meet the strict rubric — balanced double-entry with correct accounts, identity-verified idempotent replay under row/advisory locks, server-side payer-ownership and cross-estate guards, all backed by live-DB tests.
