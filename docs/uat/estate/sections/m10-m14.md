## Cross-cutting invariant verdicts (doc §4.2 / §2.3)

1. **Election secrecy — FAIL.** Estate `election_votes` stores voter_id with candidate_id in the same plaintext row (mig:20260616250000_estate.sql:82-88; service.go:244-250). Any holder of DB/service-role access — including the Election Officer surface and super admin — can associate voter to choice, violating "Nobody, including super admin, may associate voter with secret choice". A correct design already exists in-repo but unused by estate: `assoc_election_ballots_cast` (receipt, voter) vs `assoc_election_votes` (anonymous, no voter/no timestamp) in mig:20260910000000_association_elections.sql:50-77.
2. **Election lifecycle — BROKEN.** No Go route transitions draft→open→closed→tallied; only the frontend-web bridge creates 'open' elections and publishes 'tallied'. No quorum, tie-break, certification, cancellation, recount, nominations or rolls exist in the estate path.
3. **Minutes approval — PARTIAL.** Any estate_admin approves; re-upload silently overwrites and revokes approval (meetings.go:310-316); no versioning, locking, or chair-role concept.
4. **Vendor award/payout dual control — FAIL on payout.** Payout is self-service by the vendor (vendor.go:283 + handler.go:1217) with no finance-officer approval; the CRITICAL fix (quote cannot alter payout amount, vendor.go:233-238) mitigates but does not replace maker-checker. Suspension state is not enforced at assignment.
5. **Document retention/legal hold/e-signature — ABSENT.** No versioning, approval, expiry, acknowledgement, e-signature, deletion/hold or export features.
6. **Facilities RBAC scoping — FAIL vs delegated roles.** All officer duties collapse to `estate_admin` (service.go:1548); the facilities "RBAC" migration seeds only platform-admin read slugs (mig:20270226000000), not per-estate Facility Officer scoping; booking overlap is unenforced.
