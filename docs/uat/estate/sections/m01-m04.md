### Highest-severity gaps (P0, security/money first)
1. **No merchant authorization gate on estate creation/administration** — any authenticated customer can `POST /api/finance/estate` and self-become `estate_admin` (`estate/service.go:52–89`); nothing in the estate module consults onboarding approval or verified-merchant status. Undermines M01-0004/0010, M02-0021, M04-0062 and the spec's core "merchant privileges cannot be self-granted" outcome.
2. **No delegated staff-role model at all** — `estate_residents` role CHECK is only ('resident','estate_admin') (`20260616250000_estate.sql:22`); Gate/Finance/Election/Facility officer delegation, revocation and separation of duties (M04-0055…0061, 0068) are unimplementable today.
3. **Cross-estate discovery leak** — `ListEstates` (`service.go:1336`) returns every estate's name/address/admin_id to any authenticated user; directly fails M04-0054 "no sensitive response payload" and M02-0024 publish-privacy.
4. **Occupancy lifecycle is date-blind and terminal-state-less** — tenancy approval activates immediately ignoring lease_start (`service.go:1523–1540`), there is no move-out path, tenant_id is never cleared, overlapping tenants on a unit are unvalidated, and archiving skips dependency checks (`property_mgmt.go:132`). Breaks M03-0038/0044/0045/0046/0052.
5. **M02 governance layer absent** — no estate draft/publish/verify/deactivate status, no timezone, no policy versioning, no estate owner transfer, no branding, no bulk import (10 of 16 cases Missing). Requires schema additions to `estates` + admin approve/reject actions on the currently read-only platform-admin surface.
6. **Money-adjacent identity gaps** — vendor payout account stored without verification (`vendor.go:81–100`) and merchant suspension does not block privileged mutations (no status check on estate routes), while account deletion is not held on outstanding dues (`service_extras.go:301`).

- Election double-vote is DB-impossible via `UNIQUE(election_id, voter_id)` (`20260616250000_estate.sql:88`) and ballot secrecy is preserved in the admin audit read (`estate_admin_routes.go:614–645`).
