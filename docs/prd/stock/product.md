# Product — Scope, Segments, MVP, Roadmap

## What it is
A regulated, education-first **stock-only** investing product inside the Paymax super app: NGN equities, ETFs, public offers, rights issues, dividend tracking, corporate actions, portfolio analytics, beginner education, AI stock-education assistant. Two acquisition engines: existing **Paymax** users (wallet, bills, transfers, groups, subscriptions) and the **Spotlight** entertainment audience (fans, creators, contestants, youth). Goal: move users from spending/entertainment into responsible wealth creation — "from income to ownership."

**Brand promise:** *Own the companies shaping your future.* (alt: *Learn. Fund. Invest. Grow.* / *From Spotlight to shareholder.*)
**Pillars:** trust · simplicity · education · transparency · control · inclusion · culture · reliability.

## Target Segments

| Segment | Core need |
|---|---|
| Beginner investor | Plain-language onboarding, small entry, guided flow, risk warning, learn-before-trade |

## Out of Scope for MVP
Crypto · forex · options · futures · margin · securities lending · short selling · leverage · auto-copy trading · unlicensed advice · guaranteed returns · unmoderated stock-tip communities · prediction-based trading games · profit leaderboards · pump-and-dump social rooms · robo-advisory without licensing · direct management of client assets without approval.

---

## Operating Model (phased licensing)
- **Now — Partner-led:** Paymax = mobile UX + wallet funding + education + engagement + support intake + portfolio UX. Licensed **broker** executes orders; **CSDC/clearing** handles settlement; **registrars** handle dividends/corporate actions. Paymax reconciles balances vs broker. Reduces licensing/settlement/execution/regulatory/custody risk and time-to-market.
- **Later — Direct permissions:** digital stockbroking front-end · registered capital-market operator · investment distribution platform · public-offer access channel · multi-asset regulated marketplace.

## MVP Definition

**Must-have:** onboarding · KYC gating · suitability · terms + risk disclosure · stock search · stock detail · watchlist · buy · sell · order status · portfolio · investment wallet · transaction history · receipts · basic Learn Center · admin (user/stock/order/fee/limit/provider mgmt + audit logs) · settlement tracking · reconciliation dashboard.

**Should-have:** public offers · rights issues · dividend tracking · corporate actions · price alerts · AI education assistant · Spotlight Wealth Academy · support ticket integration.

**Could-have:** demo portfolio · learn-and-earn · advanced analytics · thematic watchlists · recurring buys · ETF baskets.

**Must-NOT-have:** crypto · margin · futures · options · short selling · leverage · copy trading · guaranteed returns · unlicensed advisory · profit leaderboard.

## Spotlight Integration — strict controls
Use entertainment reach for **education and trust**, never hype. **Never:** celebrity stock tips · "buy what this artist bought" · profit leaderboard · investment gambling · unmoderated trade rooms · paid stock promotion without disclosure + legal review · user-generated market manipulation. **Allowed:** Wealth Academy, creator wealth stories, "From Talent to Shareholder" campaigns, royalties-to-investing education, literacy challenges, monthly wealth livestream, public-offer awareness, learn-and-earn badges.

**Learn-and-earn rewards allowed:** wallet credits · fee discounts · badges · certificates · Spotlight points · event perks. **Not allowed:** guaranteed return · reward for risky trades · reward by profit ranking · encouraging reckless trading.

---

## Final standard
Turn ordinary users into educated shareholders, not reckless traders. The win: *"I came to Paymax to pay bills. I stayed because it taught me how to own assets."*
