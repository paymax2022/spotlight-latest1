## 3. Personal Information We May Collect

The information we collect depends on the features you use, your settings, the permissions you grant and the applicable legal requirements.

### 3.1 Personal and Account Information

When you register or use your account, we may collect:

- Full name and username.
- Mobile phone number and email address.
- Date of birth, where required for eligibility, age verification or regulatory compliance.
- Profile photograph, biography and other information you choose to provide.
- Account credentials and authentication records.
- Account preferences, settings and verification status.

We do not intend to collect more information than is reasonably necessary for the relevant service.

### 3.2 Identity Verification Information

For services that require identity verification, including regulated financial services where applicable, we may collect information such as:

- Government-issued identification details and verification results.
- Residential address and date of birth.
- Photographs or identity documents.
- Selfies or other verification materials, where required.
- Relevant compliance and fraud-prevention records.

Identity documents and biometric information, if processed, will be handled only where necessary, legally permitted and appropriately disclosed.

### 3.3 Financial and Transaction Information

If you use payment, wallet, transfer, billing or other financial features, we or the relevant financial service providers may process:

- Transaction amounts, dates, references and status.
- Wallet balances and transaction histories.
- Sender and recipient details.
- Bank account details and virtual account information, where applicable.
- Payment method details and payment confirmations.
- Relevant know-your-customer (KYC), anti-money-laundering and fraud-prevention information.

Payment card information and other sensitive payment credentials may be processed by authorised payment providers. We will disclose the relevant arrangements according to the services actually used.

### 3.4 Entertainment, Talent and User-Generated Content

If you participate in talent competitions, entertainment programmes, auditions, community activities or content-sharing features, we may collect:

- Audition and application details.
- Videos, photographs, audio recordings and other submitted content.
- Talent categories, biographies and performance information.
- Competition entries, voting records and participation history.
- Comments, messages and content you choose to publish.
- Consent and release records for the use of submitted content.

Where content is intended for public display or broadcasting, we will explain the relevant publication arrangements and obtain any consent or authorisation required by law.

### 3.5 Bookings, Events and Other Services

Where available, services such as event registration, restaurant bookings, accommodation, transport, recruitment, education or business listings may require information relevant to the requested service.

This may include booking details, attendance records, service preferences, contact information, application documents or communications with the relevant provider.

### 3.6 Device and Technical Information

When you use Spotlight SuperApp, we and our authorised technical service providers may collect certain technical information, including:

- Device type, operating system and app version.
- IP address and general connection information.
- App diagnostics, crash reports and performance data.
- Device identifiers and push notification tokens, where applicable.

### 3.7 Location Information

Some features may request access to your device location to provide relevant services, such as transport, nearby businesses or location-specific bookings.

Location access will be requested through the applicable device permission system where required.

You can generally disable location permissions through your device settings. Some location-dependent features may not work without this permission.

We do not claim to collect continuous background location unless such functionality is implemented, disclosed and appropriately authorised.

### 3.8 Camera, Microphone, Photos and Files

Certain features may request access to your camera, microphone or selected files for activities such as:

- Taking or uploading profile photographs.
- Recording or submitting audition videos.
- Participating in audio or video calls, where available.
- Uploading supporting documents.
- Creating or sharing user-generated content.

These permissions are used for the relevant feature and subject to the permissions you grant. We do not intend to access these resources for unrelated purposes without appropriate authorisation.

### 3.9 Contacts and Communications

If Spotlight introduces a feature that requires access to your contacts, we will explain the purpose and request permission where required.

We may process communications you send to our support team, including complaints, enquiries, feedback and attachments you voluntarily provide.

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable privacy laws, we process personal information on one or more lawful bases, depending on the circumstances.

These may include:

- **Consent:** Where you have freely agreed to a specific use of your information.
- **Contract:** Where processing is necessary to provide a service you requested or fulfil our contractual obligations.
- **Legal obligation:** Where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law or regulatory requirements.
- **Legitimate interests:** Where processing is necessary for legitimate operational, security or business interests and those interests do not override your rights.
- **Vital interests or other lawful grounds:** Where applicable law permits processing in specific circumstances.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it, subject to applicable law. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing lawfully performed before withdrawal.
